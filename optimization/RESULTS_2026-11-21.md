# ISMCTS decklist optimization — experimental results (2026-11-21)

## Experiment

- Pinned engine: `chorlick/alpharune@ec90f0fed002aed374d06249fa95e83e3cfbd386` plus Radiance C++ overlay (988 compiled card definitions).
- Baseline: 58 source-optimized candidate lists, with 54 unchanged source originals and four previously disclosed deck-legality corrections (Ornn, Lucian, Miss Fortune, Rengar).
- Agent: `ismcts:sims=24` with sampled information sets for both seats; separate small calibration vs uniform random agent: 10 wins / 12 games (not a general performance guarantee).
- Rules-aware mutation proposals: one main-deck copy swapped, card type and approximate mana curve matched; no changed legend, Chosen Champion, rune deck, battlefields or sideboard.
- Screened 10 swaps per deck with actual random-agent games, shortlisted 2, tested with ISMCTS in paired-seat training and independently seeded holdout games. Promoted only when both gained at least two wins. Repeated another round after an acceptance, stopped if no next swap passed.
- **58/58 decks evaluated; 5,178 real engine game evaluations; three experimental changes promoted**. Decks without a promoted candidate retain their legal baseline list.
- The experimental selected decklists completed **116/116 independent engine smoke games** against Kennen, including both seats (no errors). The tentative one-swap roster also completed 116/116.
- All source originals remain unchanged. Standard bans effective November 23 are excluded, including earlier restrictions.

## Experimental swaps and independent blind replication

Each promoted candidate was subjected to **24 further paired game scenarios** across six strong opponents on an independent seed range, both players using ISMCTS at 24 simulations per decision. The win-rate delta compares the modified deck against its fixed legal baseline on the same scenario/seating schedule.

| Legend | Single-copy change | Selection training gain | Selection holdout gain | Fresh blind win-rate delta | Paired exact sign p |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Evelynn, Agony's Embrace | −1 Corrupt Enforcer, +1 Kraken Hunter | +3/6 | +2/12 | **+25.0 pp** | 0.0703 |
| Irelia, Blade Dancer | −1 Poro Snax, +1 Scryer's Bloom | +2/6 | +4/12 | **−12.5 pp** | 0.3750 |
| Master Yi, Wuju Master | −1 Adaptatron, +1 Lee Sin, Centered | +2/6 | +2/12 | **0.0 pp** | 1.0000 |

The blind results do **not** establish statistical significance at p<0.05 for any card swap. Evelynn is the only **tentative** candidate worth additional testing; Irelia and Master Yi should remain on the legal baseline for practical recommendations. These are experiments, not reliable competitive win-rate forecasts.

## Next methodological gates

1. Require larger *pre-registered* blind samples over diverse meta decks, including current post-ban lists.
2. Verify every effect against official core rules and compare agent performance against competitively meaningful play.
3. Optimize sideboard, battlefield and rune choices. The current simulator does **not** switch sideboard cards between BO3 games.
4. Re-run an adequately sampled complete 58×58 stronger-agent matrix before replacing the previous **random-agent** leaderboard.

The current study **does not contain a new complete 58×58 ISMCTS ranking**. The 34,220 prior fixed-deck series were run with uniform random agents, and those percentages must not be relabeled as ISMCTS results.
