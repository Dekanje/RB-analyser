# Complete 58-legend ISMCTS vs ISMCTS fixed-deck BO3 study

**Run:** [GitHub Actions #44900171748](https://github.com/Dekanje/RB-analyser/actions/runs/44900171748)

## Protocol

- Pinned real C++ engine: `chorlick/alpharune@ec90f0fed002aed374d06249fa95e83e3cfbd386` + 33 added card definitions, in the same experimental branch overlay as the original benchmark.
- Roster: 54 unchanged SHA-pinned candidate decks and four **disclosed** legal candidates (Ornn, Lucian, Miss Fortune, Rengar), generated by `scripts/legal_candidate.py`. The exploratory and statistically unconfirmed optimizer swaps are not included.
- Every one of the **1,711** distinct unordered pairings, including 58 mirrors.
- **20 independent, deterministic best-of-three series per pairing**, with a total of **34,220 BO3 series** if all pass.
- In each series: keep the same deck across games; first player to reach 2 game wins is the series winner. Seating order alternates by game, and initial seating is mirrored across series.
- Both agents use `ismcts:sims=24`: information-set MCTS sampling hidden information. Search budgets are identical for both seats.
- The experiment is partitioned across 64 isolated GitHub Actions runner shards.
- Winners come only from actual AlphaRune-completed games. Timeouts, ties, engine failures, missing shards and missing series are failures; no artificial wins or imputation.
- The aggregator checks all 1,711 pairing keys, 20 series each, 64 shard reports, consistent agent and deck source, and reports per-Legend win rates and Wilson 95% intervals.
- **No main-deck/sideboard swaps between games**. This is fixed-deck BO3, not tournament-sideboarding BO3; its meta ranking is conditional on the selected 58 decks and the limited 24-simulation policy.
- Experimental card-rule implementations passed the prior regression suite, but not every individual interaction has been independently certified against the official rules. Do not present these estimates as demonstrated real-tournament win rates.
