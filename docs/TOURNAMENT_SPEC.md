# Tournament simulation — mandatory specification
Primary objective: maximize probability of winning an entire event. Configurable versioned official or hypothetical event profiles; never assume future Worlds rules or hardcode a Swiss length. Worlds without Legacy is a hypothetical configurable profile, not official fact.
## Required capabilities
TR-01 Swiss pairing by match record/points; TR-02 pairing constraints and floaters; TR-03 avoid rematches according to applicable rules; TR-04 match points/standings; TR-05 opponents' match win percentage; TR-06 game win percentage; TR-07 opponents' game win percentage; TR-08 byes; TR-09 withdrawals; TR-10 intentional draws where legal; TR-11 round time; TR-12 end-of-match procedure; TR-13 multiple days; TR-14 intermediate qualification cuts by record/points/rank; TR-15 standings carryover/reset; TR-16 top-cut qualification; TR-17 elimination bracket; TR-18 seeding and applicable play/draw advantages; TR-19 legal sideboarding; TR-20 immutable registered lists; TR-21 skill distributions; TR-22 event overrides; TR-23 replay and audit; TR-24 tournament objective optimization; TR-25 independent validation.
## Event profile fields
Ruleset/source URL/version/effective date; participant count and entrants; legal sets/banlist; round schedule and BO3 or event-specific match format; scoring W/L/D and byes; pairing policy, rematch and float rules; ordered tiebreakers, percentage floors, denominator/exclusion rules and final seeded/random resolution; clock/extra turns/draw procedures; day boundaries, qualification thresholds and carryover; top-cut size or rule, seeding bracket and playoff match policies; drops, penalties, intentional draws if allowed. Verify against exact official event documents before labeling profile official. Do not infer official rules from common TCG conventions.
## Execution
Each player locks registered deck and legal pool for event; BO3 sideboarding only via observed information and applicable decklist visibility policy. Swiss rounds pair eligible active players by official policy, execute results and update standings; cut after configured round, preserve/reset records as prescribed; seed playoffs from final standings; progress bracket to champion. Full native match mode plus fast statistical mode sampling joint game scores/draws/duration/skill distributions from validated matchup evidence, never independent binary wins alone when tiebreakers require game scores. Propagate uncertainty and cross-validate against full mode. Explicit invalid/unsupported outcomes.
## Required fixtures and acceptance
Odd/even fields, byes and repeated bye constraints, score groups/floaters/rematch avoidance, withdrawals, draws/timeouts, tiebreak denominators/floors/order, ties at cut boundary, multi-day carry/reset, Top 4/8/16/32 brackets as applicable, seeding, locked registration and sideboard legality, determinism, all players accounted for, exactly one champion for completed single-elimination event. Compare known official example pairings/standings when available; independent reference tests. Test uncertainty and distributions against full native simulation. No profile called official until sourced and tested.
Outputs: per-legend Swiss record and placing distribution, Day 2/top-cut/final/title probabilities, uncertainty intervals, field and skill assumptions, rules/engine/agent hashes and replay. Optimize qualification, top cut and championship separately; championship primary, robust over plausible fields. Basic tournament skeleton immediately after BO3 Phase 9; full championship validation Phase 15.
