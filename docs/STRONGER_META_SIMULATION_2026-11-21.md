## Explicit limitations

This run is *not* proof of optimized sideboarding. The deterministic
seeded-one-swap selector is an experimental, **non-strategic policy** whose
purpose is to exercise the real post-board engine path. It may produce
strategically poor swaps. A legal sideboard is not automatically an optimal
sideboard. The selected swap is re-evaluated after every game; the original
registered deck remains intact.

This run is also *not* a completed post-Radiance deck search. The
Riot-gallery snapshot includes all **167** numbered Radiance cards, but
earlier audits found **142** without a compiled-name match in the pinned
engine. The current AlphaRune overlay adds 33 definitions for cards needed
by the 58 original decks, including nine Radiance legends; that is not
mechanical coverage or certification for the full set. Provisional behavior,
unimplemented keywords and special edge cases mean the results are not
tournament win probabilities. The engine does not reproduce every official
between-game Battlefield-selection and competitive procedure.

The baseline roster consists of 54 unchanged original SHA-pinned decks and
four **disclosed rules-legal corrections** (Ornn, Lucian, Miss Fortune,
Rengar). The source list and every correction remain auditable.

The official Tournament Rules dated 9 October 2026 permit main/side
exchanges between games (but not before game one). A Constructed sideboard
contains up to 10 cards. Source:
https://playriftbound.com/en-us/rules-hub/
